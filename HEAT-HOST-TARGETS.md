---

## The filter (all four must hold)
1. **Off the gas grid** — oil / LPG / electric (not mains gas). Your discounted heat is worth more the dearer their fuel.
2. **Continuous, year-round heat** — process heat or 24/7 hot water, not seasonal space heating only.
3. **Low-to-moderate temperature** — compute heat is ~45–65°C. Great for hot water, space, pools, livestock, aquaculture. *Not* for >70°C process (needs a heat pump).
4. **A fast decision-maker** — a private operator/owner, not a slow procurement chain.

**Fuel value (how much your heat is worth):** mains gas ~6–7p ✗ · **oil ~8–9p** · **LPG ~10–13p** · **electric ~25p** ★. Rank every prospect by this.

## Start here — your warm channels (access beats everything for the first site)
You have **four** warm routes in — rare, and the whole game:

1. **Stone House** — your father's home, **confirmed oil**, 35 beds ≈ your £100k. The easiest first conversation; pitch the **~£10,900/yr oil saving**. Add roof solar to hit ~21-month payback.

2. **Poultry / broiler houses — your old biomass-heating business.** The best *physical* fit and a warm network:
   - **Huge, low-temperature, near-continuous heat** (20–34 °C brooding) — a perfect match for ~50 °C compute water, **no heat pump**, high capture. One house (~150–300 kW) dwarfs your £100k (16 kW), so you're **never heat-constrained**.
   - **The houses you fitted biomass into likely already have a *wet* heat-distribution system** (pipework + emitters) — so plumbing compute heat in is far cheaper than a cold host. A real edge competitors don't have.
   - **The fuel catch:** houses *already on biomass* have cheap heat (~4–6p), so displacement value there is low. The high-value targets in your network are the **LPG/gas-heated** houses (10–13p), *or* biomass houses where the boiler is ageing / fuel-handling is a hassle and they'd welcome low-maintenance heat. **Sort your contacts by fuel + by who has solar.**

3. **Your friend's solar schools** — the only route with *cheap power + warm access* together. Filter the 400 for off-gas heating + a hot-water load.

4. **The local dairy farm** — small (1 box), but a fast on-your-doorstep test of the heat integration.

## Verify any prospect's fuel (free, 2 minutes)
- **find-energy-certificate.service.gov.uk** — non-domestic EPC → "Main heating fuel" (says oil / LPG / electric / gas).
- **findmysupplier.energy** — postcode + house number → gas meter present? (no result = off-gas).

*The same check sorts oil vs LPG vs electric for you — prioritise LPG and electric over oil, oil over gas.*
