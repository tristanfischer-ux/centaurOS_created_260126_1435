## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP: Hardware Startup Founder

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Role** | Founder, CEO, or CTO |
| **Company Stage** | Pre-seed to Series A |
| **Company Size** | 1-10 people |
| **Industry** | Consumer electronics, robotics, IoT, medical devices, industrial hardware |
| **Pain Signals** | Hiring freezes, wearing multiple hats, missed deadlines, regulatory surprises, supplier communication problems |
| **Budget** | £50-500/month for SaaS tools |
| **Geography** | UK, US, EU (English-speaking) |

### Qualifying Questions
1. "Are you currently building a physical product?"
2. "How many domains do you have in-house expertise for?" (engineering, regulatory, manufacturing, finance, legal, supply chain)
3. "How many different SaaS tools does your team use day-to-day?"
4. "Have you experienced a surprise delay or cost overrun due to something you didn't know you needed?"

### Disqualifiers
- Pure software companies (no hardware component)
- Large enterprises with established procurement teams (>50 people)
- Hobbyists not building for commercial sale

## 3. Pricing Talk Track

### When They Ask "How much?"

"We have four tiers. Most founders start on Explorer — it's completely free, no credit card. Once you're getting value from the specialists and need more AI interactions or team seats, Startup Team is £49 a month. That gives you 100 AI assists, 3 team members, and full marketplace access. Professional at £149 adds unlimited orders, 500 AI assists, API access, and voice mode. There's also a 20% discount if you go annual."

### When They Say "That's expensive"

"I understand. Let me ask — what are you currently spending on project management tools, AI subscriptions, and consultants? Most founders we talk to spend £200-500/month across separate tools, plus £150-300/hour for specialist consultants. ForgeOS replaces all of that. At £49/month, that's less than a single hour of consultant time."

### When They Say "I can just use ChatGPT"

"Absolutely, ChatGPT is great for general questions. The difference is that our specialists know your company — your stage, your industry, your specific product. They remember previous conversations, they coordinate with each other, and they're purpose-built for hardware development. Plus you get the CAD lab, marketplace, and work management tools that ChatGPT doesn't offer."

## 5. Demo Script (5 Minutes)

### Setup (30 sec)
"Let me show you what it's like to have a full leadership team from day one."

### Specialists (2 min)
1. Open the specialists page — show the 13 specialists with their personalities
2. Brief Sage (Strategy) with a quick product idea
3. Show the streaming response with contextual advice
4. Point out the "Next steps" and cross-specialist recommendations

### The Forge (1.5 min)
1. Open the CAD Lab
2. Show a concept → module decomposition → 3D model
3. Show the procurement tab with auto-generated RFQ

### Marketplace (30 sec)
1. Quick browse showing categories and search
2. Show a provider profile with ratings and booking

### Close (30 sec)
"This is what £49/month gets you — strategic advisors, a manufacturing workspace, and expert access. Want to try the free tier?"

## 6. Sales Process

### Stage 1: Discovery
- Source: Organic (content, referral, community) or outreach
- Goal: Qualify against ICP, understand their current pain
- Action: Share free tier signup link

### Stage 2: Activation
- Trigger: User signs up for free tier
- Goal: Get them to first "aha moment" (meaningful specialist conversation)
- Action: Onboarding email sequence, check in after 3 days

### Stage 3: Conversion
- Trigger: User hits free tier limits or requests team features
- Goal: Convert to Startup Team or Professional
- Action: Personal outreach offering upgrade, highlight features they've been using

### Stage 4: Expansion
- Trigger: Growing team, increasing marketplace usage
- Goal: Upgrade to higher tier, activate marketplace spending
- Action: Quarterly business review, feature roadmap preview

## 7. Key Metrics to Track

- Signups per week (free tier)
- Free → paid conversion rate (target: 5-10%)
- Time to first specialist conversation (target: <24 hours)
- Monthly churn rate (target: <5%)
- Average deal size (ARPU)
- Sales cycle length (target: <14 days for self-serve)
- NPS / satisfaction score
