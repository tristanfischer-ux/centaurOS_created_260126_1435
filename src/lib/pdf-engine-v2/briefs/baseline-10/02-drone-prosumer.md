# Consumer Cinematography Drone Brief

We are designing a 4K 60 fps consumer cinematography drone in the sub-7 kg class for prosumer filmmakers, real-estate videographers, and hobbyist pilots in the UK and EU. Folding airframe, gimballed camera, obstacle avoidance, 40-minute nominal flight time.

Target market: UK and EU consumer and prosumer pilots operating under UK CAA Open Category A1/A3 and EU EASA Open subcategory A1. Primary competitors are the DJI Air 3S, DJI Mini 4 Pro, and Autel Evo Lite+; we position on price and UK-assembled supply chain for creator economy sales.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £1,850 ex-works retail (~£3,200 recommended retail price)
- Take-off mass including battery and payload: ≤ 899 g (to stay under the C0 identification threshold and keep Open A1 over-people compliance plausible)
- External envelope folded: 225 × 120 × 90 mm; unfolded rotor tip to rotor tip: 350 mm
- Nominal cruise endurance: 40 minutes at 8 m/s with full gimbal load, -10 to +40 °C ambient
- Peak hover thrust-to-weight ratio: ≥ 2.0 at maximum take-off mass
- Video payload: 1-inch CMOS sensor, 4K 60 fps ProRes-equivalent with 10-bit 4:2:0
- Radio link range: ≥ 10 km line-of-sight at 5.8 GHz under FCC/CE power limits
- Annual production volume: 80,000 units per year

Safety and regulatory:
- UK CAA CAP 722 remotely piloted aircraft system operating requirements
- EU Regulation 2019/947 and 2019/945 Delegated Act Open Category A1/A3, C0 / C1 class-identification marking
- CE + UKCA marking, EMC Directive 2014/30/EU, Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU
- ASTM F3322 remote ID broadcast module compliance
- UN 38.3 transport tests for the lithium polymer battery pack
- EN 62133-2 battery safety
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC for any collision-avoidance actuated guards

Sub-modules expected: folding carbon-fibre airframe, 4 brushless outrunner motors + ESCs + propellers, battery management system + 4S lithium polymer pack, 3-axis brushless gimbal, 1-inch CMOS camera module, flight controller with IMU + barometer + GNSS + compass, downward + front + rear vision sensors for obstacle avoidance, 5.8 GHz digital video radio, remote ID module, controller handset.
