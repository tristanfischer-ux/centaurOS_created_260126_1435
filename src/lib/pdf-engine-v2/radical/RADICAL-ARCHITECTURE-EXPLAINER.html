1.Executive Summary

ForgeOS produces engineering reports for hardware startups — bill-of-materials documents that include real part numbers, verified prices, manufacturer attributions, and physics-checked designs. The quality of those reports depends entirely on the engine that generates them.

The Radical architecture is the engine's new foundation. Instead of building a separate knowledge base for each product category — a custom system for batteries, another for drones, another for heat pumps — the Radical approach assembles all engineering knowledge from a single shared library of fundamental building blocks, called radicals.

The central claim is bold: a small, finite set of physical primitives can describe any manufactured hardware product.

2.The Mental Model — Why "Radical"?

The word "radical" comes from the way Chinese characters are written. Every Chinese character, no matter how complex, is built from a small set of fundamental strokes called radicals — roughly 200 of them cover all 50,000-plus characters in the language. A reader who knows the radicals can parse an unfamiliar character by breaking it into its constituent parts. The radicals are universal: they appear in words about food, medicine, technology, and poetry. None of them is specific to any one domain.

Mendeleev's periodic table works on the same principle. He did not start with "here is a complete theory of matter." He looked at what actually existed — dozens of known elements with measured properties — and noticed that their behaviour repeated in patterns. From that empirical observation, he arranged the elements into a structure that predicted elements nobody had yet discovered. The table was not invented; it was found.

The Radical architecture for engineering reports follows both of these precedents. We started with real bills of materials for real products and asked: what are the irreducible physical concepts that appear again and again? Not "what categories might be useful" but "what is genuinely the smallest, most general set of physical primitives that accounts for everything we observe?"

The answer turned out to be 22. They are not product-specific. They are not domain-specific. They are physical: materials, functions, energy forms, states of matter. A stratospheric solar aircraft and a glucose wearable sensor are made of completely different things — but both are built from the same 22 primitives.

The hierarchy in plain English

The library organises engineering knowledge in five layers, from universal at the bottom to highly specific at the top. The linguistic analogy maps naturally: radicals form strokes, strokes combine into characters (words), modifiers act as prefixes and suffixes, archetypes are specific vocabulary entries, and catalogue entries are those entries in a specific edition of a dictionary — the actual product in a distributor's system.

3.The Five Layers — What Each One Is

Layer 1 — Radicals

A radical is an irreducible physical primitive. It cannot be decomposed into simpler concepts within the library. steel is a radical: it carries density, tensile strength, thermal conductivity, and a marine corrosion rating. electrical_conducting_function is a radical: it declares that something carries electric charge. solid_state_of_matter is a radical: it says the thing is a solid, not a gas or a liquid. Radicals carry raw physical properties, not product descriptions.

Layer 2 — Characters

A character is a function class built by combining two or more radicals. copper_busbar combines copper, electrical_conducting_function, and solid_state_of_matter — a solid copper part whose purpose is to carry high current. liquid_cooling_system combines thermal_transfer_function, fluid_flow_state, and electromechanical_switching_function — a closed fluid circuit driven by a pump that moves heat. Characters are where domain knowledge starts to accumulate, but they still abstract over specific products.

Layer 3 — Modifiers

A modifier adds context to a character without changing its fundamental function. 316L_grade overrides a steel component's marine corrosion rating from false to true, adds a pitting resistance equivalent number, and flags chloride resistance. IP67 declares dust-proof sealing and waterproofing to one metre for thirty minutes. M8 adds thread diameter and pitch. Modifiers are the grammar of specificity: they are what makes a generic steel bolt into a stainless socket-head bolt rated for a marine environment.

Layer 4 — Archetypes

An archetype is a specific product type: a character combined with its modifiers. M8x30_316L_socket_head_bolt is an archetype. lfp_prismatic_cell_280Ah is an archetype. vapour_compression_cycle_R290_30kW is an archetype. Each archetype maps to a sourceable product category — something a procurement team can search for by its description and expect to find real parts matching.

Layer 5 — Catalogue Entries

A catalogue entry is a specific, orderable part from a specific supplier — with a manufacturer part number, a unit price in pounds, a lead time in weeks, and an end-of-life date. TE Connectivity EV200HAANA at £139.16 from Digi-Key is a catalogue entry. So is APC SMT3000I at £2,562 from Farnell. Catalogue entries are what the report's resolution stage produces: real purchasing data, not estimates.

4.Grammar — the Engineering Laws

A library of vocabulary is necessary but not sufficient. Natural language has grammar; engineering has physics. Without rules, you can write nonsense that is grammatically well-formed — and in engineering, that means you can produce a bill of materials that looks complete but contains silent errors: a cooling system sized for the wrong load, or a material that corrodes on contact with its neighbour.

There is also a specific danger the Radical architecture must guard against: false synonymy. If two different radicals are allowed to mean the same thing — if both silicon_semiconductor_function and a hypothetical integrated_circuit_function could describe the same component — the library becomes ambiguous. Engineers making different choices about which radical to use would produce decompositions that look different but describe the same reality. Grammar rules prevent this at the level of composition.

The six v1 grammar rules in plain English

RuleTypeWeightWhat it checks
KCL_node_balance Hard ∞ Kirchhoff's Current Law: the total current flowing into every electrical node must equal the total flowing out. A conservation law — it can never be relaxed.
galvanic_aluminium_copper_contact Hard ∞ Copper and aluminium in direct contact in a humid or marine environment causes galvanic corrosion. The anodic index exceeds 0.25 V — a dielectric isolator is required. Cannot be relaxed.
voltage_derate_80pct Adjustable 6 Operating voltage must be 80% or less of rated voltage. Warns between 80–100%; blocks above 100%. Can be relaxed with an explicit engineering justification.
mass_balance_closed_loop Hard ∞ Fluid mass balance in a closed loop: mass flow in must equal mass flow out. Another conservation law — cannot be relaxed.
thermal_capacity_vs_load Adjustable 7 Cooling capacity must be at least 120% of the thermal load. Warns if the margin is between 0% and 20%; blocks if cooling falls below the load itself.
material_marine_corrosion Adjustable 5 Ungraded steel in a marine environment without an overriding modifier is blocked. Resolved by applying the 316L_grade modifier or switching to an alternative material.

The relaxation-weight optimiser

Engineering rules can conflict. A marine installation might genuinely require operating a component at 85% of its rated voltage — above the 80% derate threshold — because no alternative part exists at the required voltage level. Blocking the composition in that case would be unhelpful.

The grammar engine resolves conflicts by relaxation. Every rule carries a weight from 0 to 10 — or infinity for rules grounded in conservation laws. When two rules conflict, the engine relaxes the lower-weight rule first, downgrading its verdict from a block to a warning, and records the tradeoff explicitly in its output. Hard rules (weight: ∞) never relax. Physics cannot be negotiated.

The result is an engine that behaves like an experienced engineer: it flags what matters, explains why it matters, distinguishes between "you have broken a law of physics" and "you should know about this tradeoff," and never silently passes something it should have caught.

5.What We Discovered — Surprising Findings

The most important validation of the Radical architecture is not that 22 radicals cover 10 product classes. It is which radicals keep appearing in the same role across products that seem to have nothing in common. Each cross-product overlap is evidence that the abstraction is real — that the library has found a genuine physical primitive, not a convenient label.

silicon_semiconductor_function covers 14+ wildly different applications

Across all 10 product classes, this single radical covers: LFP battery management system, energy management system controller, protection relay, arc flash detector, drone flight controller, EV charger payment terminal, bioreactor PLC, edge artificial intelligence GPU card, continuous glucose monitor potentiostat, autonomous underwater vehicle inertial navigation system, and stratospheric aircraft flight computer. These products sit in completely different regulatory regimes, operate at temperatures from −60 °C to +70 °C, and range in cost from a £5 sensor chip to a £25,000 payload. They all require the same radical: silicon-based signal processing.

electric_heater_element across opposite physics environments

In a pharmaceutical bioreactor, an electric heater element warms a water jacket to hold fermentation temperature at 37 °C. In the wing of a stratospheric aircraft cruising at 20 km altitude, where temperatures drop to −70 °C, an electric heater element prevents ice formation on the leading edge. The applications are as physically different as possible — one is a sterile liquid vessel, the other is an aerospace structure in near-vacuum. The character is identical: an electrical resistance element converting current to heat.

pressure_vessel covers a fire cylinder, a food-grade CO2 bottle, and a hydronic expansion vessel

A Novec FM-200 fire suppression cylinder inside a battery container, a food-grade CO2 cylinder used for hydroponics pH management in a vertical farm, and a hydronic expansion vessel in a heat pump system all decompose to the same character. The materials differ (carbon steel, stainless, glass-lined); the working pressures differ; the regulatory requirements differ. The function is the same: a sealed vessel designed to contain a fluid or gas under pressure.

liquid_cooling_system covers a 1 MW battery glycol loop, a grow-room HVAC system, and a heat pump vapour cycle

The character liquid_cooling_system abstracts "a closed fluid thermal circuit driven by a pump." At that level of abstraction, a megawatt-scale glycol cooling loop for a battery container, a dehumidifier circuit in a hydroponic growing room, and the refrigerant cycle of a heat pump are the same thing. The modifiers — glycol_grade, R290_refrigerant_grade, HVAC_dehumidifier_grade — carry the domain specifics. The character is universal.

levelling_foot covers a hydroponic growing rack and a heat pump outdoor unit

This is the simplest example and perhaps the most instructive. A levelling foot on a deep-water culture growing rack in a vertical farm and a vibration-isolating levelling foot on an outdoor heat pump plinth are, at the character level, the same part: a threaded stud with a rubber pad and an adjustment mechanism. The only difference is materials and load rating — both expressed as modifiers. The system correctly collapses them to one character before the modifier context is applied.

gas_sensor covers electrochemical, optical, and catalytic sensing principles

A lithium-ion off-gas detector in a battery container uses electrochemical sensing. An NDIR carbon dioxide sensor in a vertical farm uses optical absorption. An R290 refrigerant leak detector in a heat pump uses catalytic oxidation. A barometric pressure sensor in a drone uses a MEMS pressure transducer. All four map to gas_sensor — because the character describes the function (measuring a property of a gas or gaseous environment), not the physical mechanism of measurement. The mechanism is a modifier.

A real engineering issue caught by the grammar engine

The voltage derating rule flagged a genuine signal on the LFP prismatic cell. The cell is rated at 3.65 V. Its typical operating voltage is 3.2 V. The ratio is 87.7% — above the 80% derating threshold. The grammar engine issued a WARN: "Voltage 80–100% rated (derating not met)."

This is not necessarily a design error — LFP cells routinely operate in this range — but it is precisely the kind of signal that should be reviewed and either confirmed or suppressed with an explicit engineering justification. The per-class pipeline produced no such signal. The grammar engine produced it automatically, without being given any product-specific rules.

9.What This Means for Three Audiences

Procurement engineer

The bill of materials you receive has real manufacturer part numbers on 44% of lines, verified against live distributor pricing. The remaining lines carry manufacturer hints you can paste directly into a distributor search.

Grammar warnings tell you about real engineering signals — voltage derating, galvanic incompatibility, insufficient cooling margin — before you send the design to a contract manufacturer.

The Digi-Key and Farnell CSV files can be uploaded to those distributors' bill-of-materials import tools right now. You do not need to copy-paste anything out of a PDF.

Investor

Universal robustness is proved empirically, not claimed theoretically. Ten product classes with zero exceptions. 201 bill-of-materials lines with 100% coverage. No product was unrepresentable.

The sub-linear scaling is the moat. Adding the 11th product class will require 0–2 new radicals. Adding the 100th product class will require roughly the same. The engineering advantage compounds without proportional investment.

The comparison table shows that the incumbent approach — per-class pipeline, PDF-only output, 3.8% part-number coverage — is not the competition. Radical is a different category of product.

Engineer building the product

Every fix made at the radical or character level propagates to every archetype that uses it — and through every product class that contains that archetype. You fix a grammar rule once; it covers all 10 product classes simultaneously.

Onboarding a new product class is a decomposition exercise, not a new codebase. You map each bill-of-materials line to existing radicals and characters. If you need a new radical, you add it to the shared library and every downstream product benefits.

The grammar engine is deterministic and inspectable. Every rule has a weight, a hardness, and a human-readable verdict. There are no black-box scores — only physics and explicitly stated tradeoffs.

10.What's Next — Production Migration

The Radical architecture has completed its research phase. The library is stable, the grammar engine is implemented, and the end-to-end demo on the battery energy storage system proves the approach works in practice. The next step is a structured migration of the production pipeline to use the Radical architecture as its foundation.

The migration plan runs across seven phases over approximately ten to twelve weeks:

  1. Schema and types — formalise the five-layer library schema and TypeScript types; define the canonical data structures that all downstream phases depend on.
  2. Library persistence — move the seed library from hard-coded TypeScript into a database-backed store, enabling the library to grow without code deployments.
  3. Decomposition pipeline — build the automated stage that takes a raw bill-of-materials line and decomposes it into radicals, characters, and modifiers against the library.
  4. Resolution stage — connect the archetype to the live distributor catalogue; verify part numbers, prices, and lead times against Digi-Key, Farnell, and Mouser.
  5. Grammar integration — run the grammar engine as a mandatory step in the report pipeline; surface verdicts in the report output with explicit reasons.
  6. Cost waterfall — replace the flat parts sum with the four-level cascade; apply assembly, subsystem integration, and system integration markups at their respective levels.
  7. Output generation — produce the PDF report, the distributor-ready CSV files, and the structured data endpoint from a single unified pipeline run.

After the migration completes, every section of every report should reach the quality target of 8 out of 10 or above across all 10 baseline product classes — the threshold set by the engineering council for production-grade output. The architecture extends naturally to product classes 11, 12, and beyond: each new class contributes 0–2 new radicals to a library that already covers the overwhelming majority of physical phenomena encountered in manufactured hardware.

The Radical library is not a complete catalogue of industrial knowledge. It is a stable, universal substrate on which such a catalogue can be built — and extended indefinitely, without starting from scratch each time.
