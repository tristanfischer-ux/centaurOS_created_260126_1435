# Discovery-Call Brief — AI-Compute-Site Operators

*Use for any operator/partner call: Deep Green, Era4, Pulsant, nLighten, Hemiko, 1Energy, Zendo, Digital Reef.*

---

## The one goal
Find out **whether and how they work with site developers**, what they actually need, and — the real test — **whether they'll pay or formally partner for originated sites**, or just want free introductions. You are qualifying *them* as much as pitching yourself.

## Open with (30 seconds)
"I founded Lumicity — we financed, built and operated 500 megawatts of solar and 30 megawatts of biomass heating across UK farms. I'm now applying that energy-development background to compute sites." Keep it short; they live in energy, they'll get it.

## The four questions — and how to read the answers

**1. "Is your main bottleneck finding sites, or selling the compute?"**
- *Sites / install* → 🟢 you're useful — they need what you make.
- *Demand / sales* → 🔴 they can't fill what they have; more sites don't help. Wind down politely or move to the next target. (This is the demand-saturation risk that killed the operator play.)

**2. "How do you work with people who bring you sites — a fee, a joint venture, an ongoing share, or do you only deal direct with hosts?"**
- *They pay / partner developers* → 🟢 the model works; get the structure.
- *"We deal direct with hosts and invest everything ourselves"* (Deep Green's public model) → 🟠 your value has to be a **pre-qualified, exclusively-locked pipeline they can't reach alone**, not an introduction. Ask directly: "what would make you pay for that?"

**3. "What's your site spec — power/connection size, location, and do you want single-facility heat hosts or heat-network offtake?"**
- Tells you if your sites fit. Era4 and Deep Green are drifting to **network-scale + brownfield**; your sub-10-megawatt farm sites are a softer fit there — calibrate live.

**4. "What do you most need from a partner right now?"**
- Let them name the gap, then show how you fill it. Don't pre-empt.

## Hold back
The specific farms / schools / care homes and the exact economics. **Lead with energy-development credibility + heat-network offtake capability.** Play the named-pipeline card only once you know their priority *and* that they'll pay or partner.

## Flags
- 🟢 Hiring site/development roles · asks about your pipeline · describes a fee/JV structure · bottleneck is sites.
- 🔴 Only wants free intros · demand-constrained · invests everything and deals direct (no developer role) · only wants 50–500 megawatt hyperscale (you're sub-10).

## Close with
"Given that, the kind of site I can bring is [one line]. If that's useful, what's the right next step — shall I bring you a specific shortlist under a short exclusivity or heads of terms?" → move toward **locking an arrangement *before* you reveal sites.**

## Walk away knowing
**Will this operator pay or formally partner for originated, de-risked sites?**
- Yes → develop and lock a pipeline for them.
- No → it's a free-introduction relationship at best; spend your time on the ones who'll pay.
