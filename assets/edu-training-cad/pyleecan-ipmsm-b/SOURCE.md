This is a parametric two-dimensional interior permanent-magnet synchronous
motor definition. It includes the 48-slot stator, V-shaped rotor magnet
geometry, winding, dimensions, and materials. Pyleecan can regenerate its
electromagnetic geometry and finite-element mesh.

The upstream description says the benchmark is based on a 2004 Toyota Prius.
Use it to validate a universal motor-family generator, not as a silhouette to
copy into a customer design.
