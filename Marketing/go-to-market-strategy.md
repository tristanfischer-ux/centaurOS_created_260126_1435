# ForgeOS — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Last Updated**: 10 March 2026
**Status**: Draft v1

## 1. GTM Overview

### Objective
Acquire the first 122 paid subscribers (conservative), stretch target 200 within 12 months through a content-led, community-driven approach targeting hardware startup founders.

### Positioning Statement
"For hardware startup founders who need expertise across too many domains to hire for, ForgeOS is the AI operating system that provides 13 specialist advisors, a concept-to-RFQ manufacturing workspace, and a marketplace of vetted experts — unlike generic AI tools or fragmented SaaS stacks, ForgeOS understands hardware development workflows and grows with your company."

## 2. Channels & Tactics

### Tier 1: Owned Channels (High Priority)

**Content Marketing / Blog**
- Weekly blog posts targeting hardware founder pain points
- SEO-optimised for long-tail keywords (see content calendar)
- Topics: hardware development pitfalls, regulatory guides, manufacturing tips, founder stories
- Distribution: LinkedIn, Twitter, hardware communities, Hacker News

**Product-Led Growth**
- Free tier with no credit card required
- In-product upgrade prompts at natural expansion points (AI limits, team seats)
- Onboarding email sequence (7 emails over 14 days)
- Feature announcements via in-app notifications

**Email Marketing**
- Newsletter for hardware founders (weekly or biweekly)
- Drip campaigns for free-tier activation
- Upgrade nudge sequences at usage thresholds

### Tier 2: Earned Channels (Medium Priority)

**Community Engagement**
- Hardware startup subreddits, Hacker News, Product Hunt
- LinkedIn groups for hardware founders and engineering leaders
- Maker community forums and Discord servers

**Partnerships**
- Hardware accelerators: HAX, Highway1, Bolt, Techstars hardware tracks
- Maker spaces and fab labs
- Engineering education programs and bootcamps
- Industry associations (electronics, robotics, IoT)

**PR and Thought Leadership**
- Founder interviews on hardware podcasts
- Guest posts on hardware-focused publications
- Conference talks at hardware meetups and summits

### Tier 3: Paid Channels (Low Priority Initially)

- LinkedIn Ads targeting hardware founders/CTOs (test with small budget)
- Retargeting for website visitors who didn't convert
- Sponsorship of hardware newsletters/podcasts

## 3. Launch Plan

### Pre-Launch (Now - Launch Day)
- [ ] Polish core three features (specialists, CAD Lab, marketplace)
- [ ] Record 3-5 minute product demo video
- [ ] Prepare 5 launch blog posts (queue for weekly release)
- [ ] Seed marketplace with 20-50 providers
- [ ] Set up analytics (signup tracking, feature engagement, conversion funnels)
- [ ] Prepare Product Hunt assets (if using PH)

### Launch Week
- [ ] Publish launch blog post
- [ ] Email existing contacts and network
- [ ] Post on LinkedIn, Twitter, hardware communities
- [ ] Submit to Product Hunt (if ready)
- [ ] Outreach to hardware journalists and bloggers

### Post-Launch (Weeks 2-12)
- [ ] Weekly blog post cadence
- [ ] Monitor and respond to community discussions
- [ ] Iterate on onboarding based on where users drop off
- [ ] First case study from an early customer
- [ ] Accelerator partnership conversations
