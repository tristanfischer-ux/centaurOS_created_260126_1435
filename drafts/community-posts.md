## 2. Hacker News — Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: ForgeOS — Operating system for hardware startups (AI agents + marketplace of experienced professionals)

**Body:**

I have been running startups for 26 years (software and hardware, including a large-scale vertical farm). I built ForgeOS because hardware startups have a structural problem that software startups do not: you need expertise across a dozen domains simultaneously — engineering, manufacturing, finance, legal, fundraising, strategy — and pre-revenue, you cannot afford any of it.

ForgeOS combines two things:

**1. A marketplace of experienced professionals** — people with decades of domain expertise who want to work with hardware startups. They set their own day rates and founders engage them directly. Project-based, flexible engagements.

**2. 13 domain specialist AI agents** that collaborate across strategy, engineering, finance, legal, manufacturing, sales, marketing, supply chain, product, HR, fundraising, and operations:

- Concept packages from product descriptions: structured engineering overview, materials analysis, matched suppliers
- Investor intelligence: 7,800+ investors with AI matching, 49,000+ contacts, 3,000+ grants
- 13,700+ UK and European manufacturers indexed with capability data
- Team management and project tools for remote team alignment
- Business operations: strategy, objectives, cash burn, P&L, board packs

The AI agents fill expertise gaps — the experienced professionals bring depth. The design is deliberately human-centred: AI for breadth, people for depth.

Stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, multi-LLM (Anthropic Claude + DeepSeek + OpenAI for embeddings). The specialist agents use a personality system with benchmarked quality scores across actionability, specificity, strategic depth, and voice consistency.

→ www.fractionalforge.app (free tier, no credit card required)

I would welcome feedback on the approach, and I am also looking for experienced hardware professionals who want to join the marketplace.
