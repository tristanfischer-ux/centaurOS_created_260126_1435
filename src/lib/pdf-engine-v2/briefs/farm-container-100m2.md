# Containerised Vertical Farm — UK Leafy Greens, 100 m² Canopy

A modular, containerised vertical farming system comprising a 40-foot high-cube growing container with mobile LED-integrated trolleys and a 20-foot fertigation container. Intended for UK commercial farmers, agricultural businesses, and food producers who want a turnkey indoor growing system independent of weather and soil.

## Target customer

Commercial farmers, agricultural businesses, and food producers in the UK who want resilient, local supply chains for leafy greens (lettuce, basil, rocket, pak choi, kale — no fruiting crops in v1).

## Key constraints

- **Growing surface area: 100 m² total canopy across the trolleys** (this is the brief-pinned target — do not scale it up)
- Primary container: 40-foot high-cube ISO shipping container — external 12,190 × 2,440 × 2,900 mm (W × D × H)
- Secondary container: 20-foot ISO shipping container dedicated to the fertigation skid (separate envelope)
- 8 mobile growing trolleys, each 1.2 m × 2.0 m × 2.4 m, with 5 vertical tiers per trolley (8 × 1.2 × 2.0 × 5 ≈ 96 m² canopy — round to 100 m² target)
- Air-handling unit volumetric flow rate: 3,000–4,500 m³/h (20–30 ACH)
- DX cooling capacity: 18–25 kW peak refrigeration (size against the LED heat load + auxiliary loads with safety margin)
- Dedicated dehumidification: chilled-coil with reheat OR desiccant rotor
- Condensate recovery loop returning to fertigation tank
- CO₂ enrichment to 800–1200 ppm during photoperiod
- Power supply: 3-phase 63A (~44 kW)
- Target yield: ~25 tonnes fresh-weight per year (leafy greens, 16 h/day photoperiod)
- Crop types: lettuce, basil, rocket, pak choi, kale
- Target temperature: 20–24 °C
- Target humidity: 60–75 % RH
- Lighting: tunable-spectrum horticultural LED at 200–300 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ PPFD per tier (commercial leafy-greens range)
- Energy use: closed-loop water + envelope-insulated to minimise tonne-per-m² operating cost

## Safety and regulatory

- BS EN 60204-1 (electrical safety of machinery)
- EN 60335-2-76 (safety of water-contact appliances, applicable to nutrient pumps)
- Food contact materials — BS EN 1186 / EU 10/2011 for any plastic in direct contact with irrigation water
- BRCGS Global Standard for Agriculture (for customers selling into supermarkets)
- WRAS approval for any mains water connection components
- CE / UKCA marking for completed product
- BS EN ISO 1496-1 (ISO shipping container dimensional standard)

## Sub-modules expected

Container shell + mobile growing trolleys; LED lighting array + drivers; fertigation tank + pumps + dosing; circulation fans + HEPA filter; HVAC / dehumidifier; CO₂ dosing; environmental sensor stack (temp, RH, CO₂, EC, pH, PAR); PLC + HMI; power distribution and safety interlocks; trolley castor / floor guide rail / utility quick-connect docking system.

## Why now

Increasing demand for local, resilient food production and efficient use of space, coupled with advancements in LED and climate control technologies, makes modular controlled-environment agriculture economically viable for UK supermarket and food-service supply.
