# High-Altitude Pseudo-Satellite (HAPS) Brief

We are designing a 50-metre-wingspan solar-electric high-altitude pseudo-satellite (HAPS) for 30-day continuous stratospheric persistence at 18 to 20 km altitude, providing regional communications relay, ISR (intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance) payloads, or Earth observation services. UK-assembled airframe, UK-based mission operations centre.

Target market: UK Ministry of Defence (MoD Digital Backbone programme, Royal Air Force persistent-ISR requirements), UK Space Command (stratospheric tier of the hybrid space architecture), and commercial mobile network operators (Vodafone, BT) piloting non-terrestrial 5G relay. Direct competitors are Airbus Zephyr S, BAE PHASA-35 and AeroVironment Sunglider.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £5,200,000 ex-works per airframe, excluding mission payload
- Wingspan: 50 m; total aircraft mass: ≤ 165 kg including 25 kg mission payload
- Cruise altitude: 18 to 20 km pressure altitude; minimum safe altitude for piloted traffic separation 17 km
- Endurance target: 30 days continuous flight at 4 °N to 60 °N latitude band year-round
- Solar array: ≥ 55 m² active crystalline silicon at 24 % efficiency
- Battery storage: ≥ 40 kWh lithium-sulphur or high-specific-energy Li-ion (> 350 Wh/kg pack-level)
- Cruise power: ≤ 350 W at cruise, ≤ 1,200 W peak at climb-to-altitude
- Airspeed: 50 km/h nominal cruise; max ground-referenced wind tolerance 25 m/s at altitude
- Operating temperature: -75 to +50 °C airframe, -40 to +60 °C electronics
- Payload power budget: 150 W continuous, 400 W peak for 10 % duty cycle
- Annual production volume: 12 airframes per year

Safety and regulatory:
- EASA Special Condition for HAPS (SC-HAPS) certification basis
- UK CAA CAP 722A HAPS operations requirements
- ITU Radio Regulations for any mission-payload spectrum (typically Ka / C / S band)
- EUROCAE WG-112 HAPS standards working group guidance
- UK MoD DEFSTAN 00-970 Part 16 unmanned aerial system airworthiness (military variant)
- Lithium battery transport UN 38.3 + IATA DGR Special Provision A99
- Environmental qualification: MIL-STD-810H for thermal cycling, vibration, low-pressure
- Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU for communications payload (commercial variant)
- IP55 sealed electronics bays; all conformal-coated PCBs; radiation-tolerant GPS + IMU (Kintex-7 / GR716 class)

Sub-modules expected: carbon-fibre composite main spar + secondary spars, ultralight Mylar wing skin, monocoque fuselage pod, 2 pusher propellers with brushless motors + variable-pitch hubs, 4 × control surface servos (ruddervator + spoiler + flaperons), 55 m² solar array (c-Si panels + MPPT charge controllers), battery management system + 40 kWh pack, flight control computer with redundant IMUs + GPS, payload bay with modular wet / dry compartments, Ka-band communications payload + steerable antenna, Iridium backup telemetry, de-icing + heater pads for battery pack, radiation-shielded flight-critical electronics, mission operations ground station + spectrum / airspace management workstation.
