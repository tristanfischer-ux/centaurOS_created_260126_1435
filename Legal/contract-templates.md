## 3. Employment & Contractor

### Employment Contract (UK)
**Use**: Future UK employees

Required terms (Employment Rights Act 1996):
- Job title and description
- Start date and probation period
- Salary and benefits
- Working hours and location
- Holiday entitlement (minimum 28 days inc. bank holidays)
- Sick pay provisions
- Notice period
- Confidentiality and IP assignment
- Non-compete and non-solicitation (reasonable scope)
- Grievance and disciplinary procedures

**Status**: Not yet needed — draft before first hire.

### Contractor Agreement
**Use**: Freelance developers, designers, consultants

Key clauses:
- Scope of work and deliverables
- Payment terms and rates
- IP assignment (all work product assigned to Fractional Forge)
- Confidentiality
- Status (independent contractor, not employee)
- Termination
- No IR35 / employment status issues (ensure genuinely self-employed)

**Status**: Template needed before engaging contractors.

## 5. Vendor & Partner

### Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
**Use**: With all sub-processors handling personal data

Required contents (GDPR Art. 28):
- Subject matter, duration, nature of processing
- Types of personal data and categories of data subjects
- Obligations of processor
- Sub-processing permissions and notifications
- International transfer mechanisms
- Data breach notification procedures
- Audit rights
- Deletion/return of data on termination

**Status**: Template needed. Must be signed with Supabase, Vercel, AI providers.

### NDA (Mutual)
**Use**: Pre-partnership or pre-investment discussions

Key clauses:
- Definition of confidential information
- Permitted use (evaluation purposes only)
- Duration (typically 2-3 years)
- Exclusions (public knowledge, independently developed, etc.)
- Return/destruction of materials
- No obligation to proceed with relationship

**Status**: Template needed.
