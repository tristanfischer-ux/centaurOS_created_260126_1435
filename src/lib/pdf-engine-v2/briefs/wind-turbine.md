# UK 6 MW Onshore Wind Turbine Brief

We are developing a 6 MW onshore wind turbine optimised for low-to-medium wind speed sites in the UK and northern Europe. Configuration is a three-bladed, upwind, horizontal-axis machine with a 155 m rotor diameter and a steel monopole tower at 120 m hub height, designed for Class IIIB low-to-medium wind sites under IEC 61400-1 Ed.4.

Target customer: UK onshore wind developers (SSE Renewables, RWE, Statkraft, Octopus Energy Generation, Greencoat UK Wind) and farmer-landowner repowering projects deploying under Allocation Round 7 (AR7) and the upcoming AR8 Contracts for Difference. Operated under UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) wind-turbine guidance and the EU Network Code for Requirements for Generators (RfG, EU 2016/631).

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £4,800,000 ex-works for the nacelle + rotor + tower (excluding foundation, transformer station, grid-connection cabling, and access road)
- Rated electrical power: 6.0 MW at the medium-voltage transformer terminals (33 kV)
- Cut-in wind speed: 3.0 m/s at hub height
- Rated wind speed: 11.0 m/s (low-wind-class design point)
- Cut-out wind speed: 25.0 m/s (10-minute average), with storm-protection feathering above 28 m/s
- Survival wind speed: 52.5 m/s (3-second gust) per IEC 61400-1 Class IIIB
- Rotor diameter: 155 m (swept area 18,869 m²)
- Specific power: ≤ 320 W/m² (low specific-power design optimised for capacity factor)
- Capacity factor target: ≥ 42% at a 7.0 m/s mean annual wind speed site (UK onshore typical)
- Drivetrain: direct-drive permanent-magnet generator (no gearbox) to reduce maintenance over 25-year life
- Acoustic emission: ≤ 105 dBA sound power level per IEC 61400-11 at rated power
- Operating temperature: -20 °C to +40 °C ambient; cold-climate package (-30 °C) optional
- Service life: 25 years design life; 30 years achievable with mid-life refurbishment
- Annual production: 60 units per year by year 5 (UK + Ireland + Nordics combined)

Safety and regulatory:
- IEC 61400-1 Ed.4:2019 (wind-turbine design requirements, Class IIIB)
- IEC 61400-3-1:2019 (offshore-related load cases, used as cross-check for high-shear sites)
- IEC 61400-22:2010 (conformity testing and certification)
- IEC 61400-24:2019 (lightning protection)
- IEC 61400-25 series (communication for monitoring and control)
- DNV-ST-0376:2024 (rotor blade structural requirements)
- DNV-ST-0438:2023 (loads and site conditions)
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC + Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU
- BS EN 50308:2004 (occupational safety of wind turbines)
- UK CAA airspace lighting (aviation hazard) requirements above 90 m hub height
- EU RfG (EU 2016/631) Type D grid-code compliance for ≥ 4 MW units

Sub-modules expected: rotor blades (three 76 m epoxy-infused glass-fibre blades with carbon-fibre spar cap, segmented for road transport), pitch system (electric pitch actuators with back-up battery for emergency feathering), main bearing (single-row tapered-roller, locating type), main shaft + hub (cast SG iron hub, hollow steel main shaft), direct-drive generator (permanent-magnet synchronous, 6 MW rated, low-speed 12-15 rpm), full-power converter (medium-voltage IGBT or silicon-carbide modules with grid-side and machine-side bridges), step-up transformer (33 kV / 690 V, oil or dry-type), nacelle assembly (welded steel main frame with bedplate housing all rotating components), yaw system (active electric yaw, 4-8 yaw drives on slewing ring bearing), tower (3-section conical steel monopole, 120 m hub height, flange-bolted), foundation interface (gravity-base or rock-anchored), control system (Siemens, Bachmann, or in-house wind-turbine controller running predictive control with model-predictive control for individual pitch).
