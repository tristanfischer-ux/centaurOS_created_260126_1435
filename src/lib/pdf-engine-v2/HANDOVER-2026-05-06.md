## What got built (tonight — ~35 commits on `main`)

The pdf-engine-v2 now reliably produces 50-90 page engineering reports for three test briefs (BESS, heatpump, vertical farm) with:

- Verbatim user brief shown at the top of Section 1 (what was actually asked for)
- Real UK/EU suppliers per BOM row (from local Nightshift corpus — 13,771 embedded suppliers)
- BOM with Quantity × Unit £ = Extended £ column + module subtotals
- Cost waterfall: Raw BOM → labour → test → shipping → overheads → contingency → unit cost
- NRE broken down per regulatory standard (UL 9540A £100k, G99 £60k, etc.)
- Regulatory table with £ cost + weeks per standard
- Supplier Shortlist section with certifications + process capabilities
- FMEA with S × O × D RPN, verification tests, RPN banding
- Benchmark Comparison panel on cost waterfall — cites BNEF, Modo Energy, IRENA, HPA, supplier list prices
