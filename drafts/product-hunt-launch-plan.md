## Description (200 chars)

ForgeOS generates complete engineering packages from product descriptions — STEP files, BOM, DFM analysis, matched UK suppliers. What takes freelance engineers 4-8 weeks takes ForgeOS 3 hours. Built for hardware founders.

## Maker Comment (post immediately after launch)

Hi Product Hunt! I'm Tristan, founder of ForgeOS.

I built this because I was frustrated by how long it takes hardware startups to go from idea to manufacturing-ready specs. The "lean startup" methodology breaks down when each iteration takes 57 days.

**What makes ForgeOS different:**

1. **13 AI specialists** work together on your engineering package — a CTO, VP Manufacturing, Finance Lead, Legal Counsel, and 9 others. They challenge each other's assumptions.

2. **Real output, not just advice.** You get a product architecture, full BOM with cost modelling at 3 volumes, DFM analysis, certification roadmap, and matched suppliers from 13,700+ UK manufacturers.

3. **3 hours vs 4-8 weeks.** One of our users generated a complete engineering package for an IoT irrigation system in 3 hours. A freelance engineer quoted 6 weeks and £8,000 for the same scope.

Free tier available — no credit card required. I personally onboard every founding member.

Would love your feedback. Happy to answer any questions about the multi-agent architecture or the engineering pipeline.
