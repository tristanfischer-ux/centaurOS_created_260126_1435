Overview
This product is a residential air-source heat pump designed specifically for the UK market, delivering
8 kW of rated thermal output. It utilises R290 (propane) refrigerant to comply with EU 517/2014 F-gas
regulations and achieves a highly efficient Seasonal Coefficient of Performance (SCOP) of 3.5. The
system is engineered to integrate seamlessly with a standard 200L domestic hot water cylinder, providing both space heating and hot water for semi-detached properties. To meet stringent UK residential requirements, the unit is constrained to a maximum gross mass of 90 kg and features advanced
acoustic dampening to ensure a night-time sound pressure limit of 35 dBA at 1m. The design incorporates a variable-speed BLDC compressor, electronic expansion, and EC motor-driven fans and pumps
to maximise efficiency across an operating ambient range of -25 °C to +45 °C.

Mission
To deliver a highly efficient, R290-based residential air-source heat pump tailored for UK semi-detached homes.

Target customers
Homeowners of semi-detached properties in the United Kingdom. Installers operating under the UK
Microgeneration Certification Scheme (MCS).

The mass fluid transport process module manages the internal flow of both the R290 refrigerant and
the hydronic heating water. The refrigerant routing sub-module directs the R290 flow using a 4-way
reversing valve and a network of brazed copper refrigerant pipes, enabling both heating and active
defrost cycles. On the water side, the hydronic routing sub-module circulates water to the external 200L
cylinder using a circulation pump body rated for 200 L/h, while an 8 L expansion vessel accommodates
thermal expansion within the closed loop.
These fluid networks operate at up to 45 bar on the refrigerant side and 3 bar on the hydronic side,
ensuring efficient mass transport between the energy conversion components and the external environment. The mass fluid transport process module routes R290 through a Sanhua STF-0408G 4-way
reversing valve of 110x70 mm brass construction and Mueller ACR-3/8 phosphorus deoxidised copper
pipe of 3/8 inch OD. On the hydronic side a Wilo Yonos-PICO-200L pump body 130x80 mm circulates
water to the external 200 L cylinder while a Flamco Flexcon-8L expansion vessel of carbon steel with
butyl diaphragm measuring 280x200 mm accommodates volume changes at 3 bar maximum pressure.

Risk & Failure-Mode Analysis
Class-level pre-mitigation hazards a heat pump design must address. Each hazard is rated on three 1-5
scales whose product gives a single risk priority number — higher means worse before mitigation.
MANUAL REVIEW — G3 completeness
Review (G3) completeness gate exhausted retry budget — manual review required.

Severity — how bad the outcome is if the hazard occurs (1 = inconvenience, 5 = injury / fire / total loss).
Likelihood — how often it tends to happen in fielded systems before mitigation (1 = very rare, 5 = frequent).
Detectability — how hard it is to spot before it causes harm (1 = obvious / instrumented, 5 = silent failure).
Risk priority — severity × likelihood × detectability. The single number used to rank hazards.

Heat pump hazards centre on refrigerant containment (especially A3-class refrigerants like R290 propane),
refrigerant-circuit pressure containment, and rotating-machinery safety. Residential R290 systems balance
flammability risk against the F-gas regulatory pressure to phase out high-GWP HFCs.
