# Containerised Battery Energy Storage System Brief

We are designing a single-container, grid-tied lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery energy storage system with a nameplate energy capacity of 3 MWh and a continuous power rating of 1.5 MW (two-hour duration). The complete system is built into one 40-foot ISO container that arrives on site ready to connect, stores electricity from the grid and discharges it back on demand, providing frequency response, peak shaving and wholesale energy arbitrage. It connects to the medium-voltage network through a dedicated step-up transformer.

Target market: UK commercial-and-industrial energy users, independent power producers and network operators seeking a containerised storage block for frequency response (Dynamic Containment, Dynamic Moderation, Dynamic Regulation), peak shaving, and wholesale price arbitrage. Grid-tied deployment at 11 kV.

System description:
- Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) prismatic cells assembled into modules, modules into racks, and the racks installed in one 40-foot container
- The container holds the cells, module-level battery management, rack-level controls, and a liquid-cooling thermal-management system
- A bidirectional power conversion system (PCS) converts the battery direct current to grid-synchronised alternating current
- A step-up transformer raises the power-conversion output to the 11 kV connection voltage
- A site controller / energy-management system dispatches charge and discharge against grid frequency signals and market instructions
- Auxiliary systems: liquid cooling (chiller and coolant loop), fire detection and suppression, heating-ventilation for the electronics, and the metering and protection switchgear

Key constraints:
- Nameplate energy capacity: 3 MWh (3,000 kWh)
- Usable energy capacity: approximately 2.7 MWh at beginning of life (90% depth of discharge)
- Continuous power rating: 1.5 MW (1,500 kW), two-hour discharge duration
- Battery chemistry: lithium iron phosphate (LFP)
- Direct-current bus voltage: approximately 1,500 V nominal
- Round-trip efficiency: at least 88% at beginning of life
- Cooling: liquid-cooled battery thermal management
- Grid connection: 11 kV, three-phase, 50 Hz, through a dedicated step-up transformer
- Grid code: compliant with Engineering Recommendation G99 for connection to the distribution network
- Unit cost ceiling: £1,000,000 ex-works for the containerised system (excluding civil works and the grid connection)
- Operating environment: outdoor, ambient -20 °C to +45 °C
- Design life: 15 years, approximately 6,000 equivalent full cycles
- Footprint: a single 40-foot ISO container, transportable on a standard road trailer
- Annual production volume: 50 systems per year
- Primary objective: balanced — the lowest cost that meets the energy, power and grid-code targets

Safety and regulatory:
- UKCA marking
- Engineering Recommendation G99 for connection to the distribution network
- IEC 62619 and IEC 62933 for industrial battery safety and grid energy storage
- NFPA 855 and UL 9540 / UL 9540A for energy-storage-system fire safety and thermal-runaway propagation testing
- BS EN 62477 for the power-conversion equipment
- DSEAR assessment for vented battery gases
- Machinery Directive, Low Voltage Directive and Electromagnetic Compatibility Directive for the equipment
- BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) for the electrical installation

Sub-modules expected: LFP battery modules and racks, battery management system (cell, module and rack level), direct-current distribution and protection, bidirectional power conversion system (PCS), step-up transformer, 11 kV switchgear and protection, site energy-management controller, liquid-cooling thermal management (chiller and coolant loop), fire detection and suppression, heating-ventilation for the electronics enclosure, auxiliary low-voltage power and metering, and the 40-foot container enclosure with environmental protection.
