# Compute-heat at the solar schools — conversation prep

*One-pager for the chat with your friend (the 400-school solar business). June 2026.*

---

## The reframe that makes this concrete
Your friend's schools **already have the solar** — the cheap-power lever is largely in place. You're not looking for solar; you're looking for the **subset where the heat is worth the most**. That's a single, simple filter, and it's the whole conversation.

## Why it's a win for *his* business too (lead with this — it's a partnership, not a favour)
It bolts a second story onto his solar pitch: *"solar by day **and** waste-heat reuse warming your hot water."* That's a stronger proposition to a bursar, a new sustainability angle, and a potential revenue/partnership line for him. Frame it as something that makes **his** offer better, not just something you need from him.

## The one filter that matters — off the gas grid
| School heats with… | Heat you displace | Does it pay? |
|---|---|---|
| **Mains gas** | ~6p/kWh | barely — heat adds little |
| **Oil / LPG / electric** (usually rural) | **20–25p/kWh** | **yes** — ~£3.4k per box/yr, the line that pulls £100k to a ~22-month payback |

**So the target is the rural, off-gas-grid school** — valuable heat, and the one most likely to have the roof space his solar needs anyway. The two best levers land on the same site.

## The specific ask for your friend — three data points on the 400
1. **Heating fuel** — which are *off the gas grid* (oil / LPG / electric)?  ← *the big one*
2. **Year-round hot-water demand** — kitchens, showers, pools (a far better match for compute heat than seasonal space heating).
3. **Governance** — academies / multi-academy trusts (3–6 month sign-off) vs council-maintained (6–18 months).

## First-site checklist (where you'd put one £15k box to prove it)
- ☐ Off the gas grid (oil / LPG / electric heating)
- ☐ Solar already installed
- ☐ Continuous hot-water load of at least a few kW
- ☐ Plant room with space + a spare 16–32A circuit
- ☐ Academy / MAT (fast sign-off)
- ☐ Decent broadband — the compute needs the **fibre**, not the grid

## Timing — why now is genuinely right
Summer is the **install window** (school empty), and the decision lands **before the autumn heating season and the next budget cycle**. Good moment to open it.

## The honest positioning (for the school, later)
Start with **free heat** to win the site fast (no procurement, quick yes). At the **off-gas** ones, offer **discounted** heat instead — still a no-brainer for them, and that's the version that actually pays your £100k back.

## What a "yes" unlocks
1–2 candidate schools → put **one box** in → prove the heat integration into a real school loop and **measure the actual compute demand/utilisation** (the single number everything hinges on) → *then* scale the £100k.
