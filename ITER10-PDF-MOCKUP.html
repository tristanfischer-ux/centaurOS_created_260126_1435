System-Level Risks & Integration

Cross-cutting issues that span more than one module — checked BEFORE you read individual modules in case a cumulative effect needs the system-level view.

Cumulative power load vs site supply

Three modules each requested power independently — totals must fit the brief-specified supply.

ERP-SYS-1 SYSTEM ELECTRICAL
Total peak load 38.2 kW (Module 2 HVAC 23 kW + Module 4 LED 12 kW + Module 5 ancillary 3.2 kW) vs brief 3-phase 32A = 22 kW available
Why it matters: Even with balanced loading the brief-supplied 3-phase 32A connection will trip on peak demand.
Action: Either upsize to 63A connection (44 kW headroom) OR stage HVAC + LED dimming via PLC. Sign-off needed from electrical engineer + DNO.

Total mass & ISO container load rating

ERP-SYS-2 SYSTEM MECHANICAL
Calculated installed mass 18.7 t (modules sum) vs 40ft ISO container max gross 30.5 t — comfortable but check floor point loading under 8 trolleys
Why it matters: Floor point loading per castor > 500 kg may exceed ISO container floor rating in localised zones.
Action: Specify floor reinforcement plate spec or distribute castors per CIMC floor-load drawing.

★ Engineering Review Notes

3 items to verify before procurement, detailed design, or commissioning.

ERP-2.1 ELECTRICAL
Driver-to-load power balance — 8× Mean Well HLG-320H-48 drivers totalling 2.56 kW feeding 2.5 kW LED load per trolley = zero headroom
Why it matters: No margin for cable losses or driver derating at high ambient. The driver itself may run hot.
Action: Substitute Mean Well CSP-3000-48 (single 3 kW driver per trolley) OR upgrade to 9 drivers per trolley.
ERP-2.2 MECHANICAL
Static pressure feasibility — 300mm axial fan against 150 Pa duct static pressure typically stalls (axial max ~100 Pa)
Why it matters: Wheel surge / stall — declared 4500 m³/h is unachievable.
Action: See replacement at 2.1.3 above (centrifugal plug fan). Re-check ductwork sizing for actual static loss; this fan handles up to 500 Pa.
ERP-2.3 SYSTEM
Cross-module wiring — CO2 over-concentration safety interlock between Module 2 (HVAC exhaust) and Module 7 (CO2 sensor) wasn't explicitly specified
Why it matters: CO2 alarm at 1500 ppm needs to drive exhaust-fan ramp + CO2 solenoid close. Wiring topology not in design output.
Action: Specify alarm-interlock wiring before panel fabrication. Typical: 4-20 mA from NDIR sensor → PLC AI → safety relay → contactor.
