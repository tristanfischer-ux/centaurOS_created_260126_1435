3 · Red team · 5 critiques2 critical · 2 high · 1 medium

Structural concerns with what IS built, not what's missing. Each critique: claim · evidence · what could break · mitigation. Pulled from MemPalace red-team synthesis (drawer forgeos/decisions, 2026-04-19) + competitor research (Dovetail / Maze / Intercom Surveys).
1

Readiness-item close has no enforced evidenceAction tab will become Fundability-score theatre v2 if we don't gate "close" on evidence

CRITICAL
The Action tab replaces the killed 56/100 Fundability composite with a checklist — but the schema lets founders mark items closed with an empty evidence_ref. That recreates the exact failure mode the composite had: vibes-based self-rating. A founder preparing for an investor call will close all 12 items in 5 minutes without a single linked LOI.
Evidence: PRODUCTS-SCHEMA §2.8 — evidence_ref jsonb with no NOT NULL constraint. READINESS-ACTION mock shows "close" button with optional evidence attach. Founder can save without attaching.

What could break

  • Founder closes 10/12 readiness items before real evidence exists → opens app thinking they're "investor-ready" → pitches → rejected because none of the closed items have evidence they can show an investor.
  • Priya's synthesis ribbon reads "10/12 ready — strong position" based on counts alone, reinforcing the false confidence.
  • An investor asks "you closed 'Can you name 3 signed LOIs' — show me them" and the Action tab can't surface them because the ref was never required.
Mitigation: Hard constraint: CHECK (status != 'closed' OR evidence_ref IS NOT NULL AND jsonb_array_length(evidence_ref) > 0). Close button in UI gates on attaching at least one {entity_type, entity_id} ref. Exception: items with status = 'not_applicable' skip the check (some items genuinely don't apply — e.g., "advisor" question for a solo founder).
2

Assumption → valid transition has no experiment requirementAssumption can flip to valid without a single test attached

CRITICAL
Schema lets assumptions.status move from untested → valid with zero linked experiments. That is the same pattern as an unsourced TAM/SAM/SOM — a founder mark-ups a claim as validated without cited evidence. Investors will see through it instantly ("what experiment validated this?") but the app happily shows a green "valid" pill.
Evidence: PRODUCTS-SCHEMA §2.6 — linked_experiment_id uuid FK is NULLABLE. UI state transitions aren't gated on a linked row.

What could break

  • Founder manually flips assumptions to valid to green-light the Action tab checklist. Synthesis ribbon reads "4 of 5 assumptions validated — strong case".
  • Pitch deck says "we've validated 4 of 5 critical assumptions" based on the status column. No receipt.
  • Invalidated → valid flip (an assumption re-tests positive) requires NEW evidence; the old flip didn't. Inconsistent.
Mitigation: Gate the transition: untested/testing → valid requires linked_experiment_id IS NOT NULL AND that experiment's decision = 'keep'. Server action enforces it; UI shows "Attach an experiment first" on the valid toggle if the gate fails. invalid transition has no evidence requirement (invalidation is cheap — a single contradicting interview is enough).
3

Evidence has no half-lifeA customer interview from 8 months ago reads the same as last week's

HIGH
Per competitor research (Dovetail / Maze / Intercom Surveys), evidence decays. For pre-revenue hardware founders, an interview from 2025-Q3 is a different asset than one from 2026-Q2 — the market moves. Current Products schema timestamps every evidence item but the UI shows them identically regardless of age.
Evidence: PRODUCTS-SCHEMA §2.4 (interviews) has interviewed_at but no decay. §2.8 (readiness_items) has evidence_ref but doesn't check age of the cited entity. A readiness item can "close" citing an 8-month-old interview.

What could break

  • Founder refreshes pitch deck quarterly; cites "we talked to 12 farmers" — 9 of whom were interviewed 14 months ago. Investor pattern-matches as stale.
  • Market has shifted (new regulation, new competitor) between the interview and the pitch; the interview insight is technically wrong now but still counted.
  • Readiness checklist closes citing a stale LOI (draft > 12 months old that was never re-confirmed). Same failure mode.
Mitigation: Add a stale derived flag (computed in view or app-layer, not a stored column): interviews > 90 days = stale; LOIs > 180 days without being re-confirmed = stale; market_sizings > 180 days = stale (already in schema). Action tab readiness_items that cite only stale evidence surface a warning pill "this close relies on stale evidence". Doesn't invalidate — surfaces the risk so the founder can re-interview, re-ping, re-size.
4

Hypothesis-create form is too permissiveNo grammar enforcement; "users will love it" saves cleanly

HIGH
Per Maze research pattern, hypothesis clarity is a write-time concern, not a read-time concern. Current schema accepts a free-text problem field and a free-text one_liner. There's no "If X, Then Y, Because Z" grammar — which means founders can save "users will love it" and move on. That hypothesis is unfalsifiable and unmockably testable. The rest of the product (assumptions, experiments, readiness) assumes a testable hypothesis upstream.
Evidence: PRODUCTS-SCHEMA §2.1 — one_liner, problem are free-text columns. No CHECK constraint. No grammar template in the mock (gap 1.2 flags the missing create form, but the issue is deeper than "mock it" — it's "structurally force the grammar").

What could break

  • Migrated hypotheses (from existing products.description) come in as free-form marketing copy. None are falsifiable statements. Every downstream tab (Market / Evidence / Action) struggles to be useful because the premise is vague.
  • Founders create vanity hypotheses to fill the list view. Without grammar, "make ForgeOS better" is a valid hypothesis. Nothing stops it.
  • Priya's synthesis ribbon can't summarise a non-statement. Her output reads like horoscope copy.
Mitigation: Hypothesis-create form (gap 1.2) requires three separate fields: If (change) · Then (impact) · Because (assumption) — each non-empty. Saved into the problem column as a structured blob (e.g., JSONB {if, then, because}) OR kept separate as three columns. UI composes them back into a one_liner for display. Migrated hypotheses get {if: "(migrated from v1)", then: description, because: "(not captured)"} and an action item "Upgrade this hypothesis with testable grammar" auto-added to Action tab.
5

PROMOTE_TO_FORGE UI reads as terminalIt's reversible until Brief-Lock — but the dialog says "proceed"

MEDIUM
Promotion flips canonical_surface from products to forge. That's reversible until the Brief is locked on the Forge side. The current PROMOTE-TO-FORGE mock reads as terminal — "proceed to The Forge" with no reversibility copy. Founders will either hesitate (bad — they don't commit) or over-commit (bad — they think they're locked in). Transparency is the fix.
Evidence: PRODUCTS-SCHEMA §5.3 documents the reversible window. PROMOTE-TO-FORGE.html mock has no banner about reversibility. Founders will parse the tone as one-way.

What could break

  • Founder with a half-ready hypothesis hesitates to promote because "what if I'm wrong". Brief drafting blocked. No Forge work happens.
  • Founder promotes a weak hypothesis (because "I can always edit in Forge"), hits Brief-Lock too eagerly, then discovers the reversal is gone. Now locked into building a weak brief.
  • Without a clear "reversible until Brief-Lock" line, Tristan's own testing revealed this ambiguity during red-team round 2.
Mitigation: Promote dialog copy explicitly states: "Promoting moves canonical ownership to The Forge. You can un-promote until the Brief is locked. After Brief-Lock, this hypothesis becomes read-only in Products — the Brief is the authoritative record. You can always archive." Promote-confirmation on the Forge side surfaces the same reversibility window ("This promotion is still reversible until you lock the Brief"). Gap 6.2 flags the copy work.
