# Modular Small-Scale Lithium-Battery Recycling Line Brief

We are designing a modular, small-scale lithium-ion battery recycling line that takes end-of-life battery packs and modules, safely discharges and dismantles them, mechanically shreds them under inert/controlled atmosphere to produce "black mass", and runs a compact hydrometallurgical stage to recover battery-grade cobalt, nickel, manganese and lithium salts plus copper and aluminium fractions. The line is skid-and-container mounted so a regional operator can install recovered-materials capacity close to where end-of-life batteries arise.

Target market: UK and EU battery-recycling operators, automotive and grid-storage take-back schemes, and metals refiners seeking distributed, permit-friendly recovery capacity ahead of EU Battery Regulation recovery targets. Single-line sale ex-works; modules ship containerised and bolt together on the customer's prepared pad.

Process description (batch-continuous):
- Receive, identify and state-of-charge-check incoming packs and modules; safely deep-discharge residual energy
- Dismantle to module/cell level; remove casings, busbars and electronics for separate recovery
- Shred cells under an inert or controlled atmosphere to suppress thermal runaway and electrolyte fire risk; extract and condense electrolyte solvent
- Separate the shred into ferrous, copper/aluminium and black-mass fractions by mechanical and density/magnetic separation
- Leach the black mass in the hydrometallurgical stage; recover cobalt, nickel, manganese and lithium as battery-grade salts by selective precipitation/solvent extraction
- Treat process effluent and gas; package recovered metal salts and metal fractions

Key constraints:
- Primary output target: 1,000 tonnes/year of end-of-life battery feed processed (continuous, single-shift basis)
- Recovery targets: ≥ 90% cobalt, ≥ 90% nickel, ≥ 70% lithium, ≥ 95% copper and aluminium, to meet EU Battery Regulation material-recovery thresholds
- Black-mass yield: approximately 0.45 t black mass per tonne of cell feed
- Output products: battery-grade cobalt, nickel and manganese salts; lithium carbonate/hydroxide; copper and aluminium fractions; recovered electrolyte solvent
- Safety atmosphere: shredding under inert/controlled atmosphere; electrolyte solvent extraction and condensation; thermal-runaway detection and suppression on the in-feed and shred line
- Utilities: process water, low-pressure steam or hot water for the hydromet stage, electricity, instrument air, reagent dosing; closed-loop effluent treatment
- Maximum module footprint: containerised / skid-mounted for road transport; field-assembled on a plinth
- Design life: 15 years; corrosion-resistant wetted path for the acidic/alkaline hydromet stages
- Operating environment: indoor/covered industrial site, 5 °C to +40 °C; ATEX zoning around the shred and solvent-handling areas (flammable electrolyte)
- Unit cost ceiling: £5,000,000 ex-works for the complete line (discharge and dismantling, inerted shredder and electrolyte recovery, mechanical separation, hydrometallurgical leach and recovery skid, effluent and gas treatment, packaging, controls) — excludes the host building, permitting and civils beyond the skid boundary
- Annual production volume: 4 lines per year
- Jurisdiction: UK and EU

Safety and regulatory:
- UKCA and CE marking
- DSEAR and ATEX Directive 2014/34/EU for the shredding, electrolyte and solvent-handling areas (battery electrolyte is flammable)
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC for the shredder, conveyors, separators and process equipment
- Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU for any pressurised vessels in the hydromet and solvent stages
- COSHH for the leach reagents, electrolyte solvent and recovered-metal dusts (toxic, corrosive)
- Environmental permitting for a waste-treatment installation; hazardous-waste handling and effluent discharge consent
- EU Battery Regulation (2023/1542) material-recovery and recycling-efficiency targets
- ADR for the transport of damaged/defective end-of-life batteries to and within the site
- Thermal-runaway and fire-suppression design to recognised battery-recycling fire-safety practice

Sub-modules expected: pack/module reception, identification and safe deep-discharge, dismantling station, inerted shredder with electrolyte extraction and solvent condensation, mechanical and density/magnetic separation, hydrometallurgical leach and selective-recovery skid (precipitation / solvent extraction), reagent dosing and storage, effluent treatment and gas scrubbing, recovered-product packaging, fire-detection and thermal-runaway suppression, and the process controls and SCADA.
