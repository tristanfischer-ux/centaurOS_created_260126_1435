3 · Red team 5 critiques

Honest analysis of what's been built. Critiques 1 and 2 are inherited from the Money gap audit and apply equally to Plan. Critiques 3, 4, 5 are net-new to Plan.
1

Specialist callouts are AI-branded — violates your own CLAUDE.md ruleCopy-voice critique · inherited from money audit

High
Every Plan mockup leans on explicit AI branding — "Sage · AI · Strategy", "Cal · AI · Chief of Staff", "Leo AI cross-checked", "13 AI specialists orchestrating". The in-product CLAUDE.md rule is explicit: no "AI-powered" / "AI-generated" / "Smart" / "Intelligent" labels, no AI agent counts, no robot/brain icons. Test: would this description make sense without mentioning AI? Mostly no — the callouts are the AI's branding.
Evidence: a dedicated .role-ai CSS class with brand-orange styling highlights AI specifically. Team panel on Workspace reads "Plus 13 AI specialists orchestrating · Sage, Fang, Harper, Leo lead on your Goals". Pressure-test page describes the 5 personas as "AI personas on different LLM providers". The rule isn't marketing-optional — it's in-product policy.

Secondary issue — voice drift

  • Silicon Valley cadence: "Taking Sarah's assumptions and drafting the Goal", "Verdict emerging", "Kill · Pivot · Double down".
  • Not Tristan's voice: British, understated, specific numbers, one clear next action.
  • Risk: LLMs will generate production copy matching our mockups' Silicon Valley voice. Baking the wrong voice into 13 specialists is expensive to unwind.
Mitigation:
• Strip AI labels from all 14 mockups: kill the .role-ai class everywhere. Specialists become "Sage · Strategy" not "Sage · AI · Strategy". "Cal drafted" stays (it's description, not branding) but "AI · Chief of Staff" pill goes.
• Frame specialists as roles: "Strategy · Next action" more useful than "Sage · AI · Strategy Lead". Still routable, less AI-branded.
• Voice sample: before production copy, pull 3-5 of Tristan's LinkedIn posts and investor emails as reference. Rewrite a sample page's copy in that voice; if it lands, that's the guide.
2

Most value depends on data the founder doesn't have yetCold-start critique · inherited from money audit

High
Every mockup shows a Q2 week-6 in-flight foundry with 3 pinned Strategic Goals, 5 onboarded fractional humans, 24 active tasks, a live pressure-test, a history of 147 decisions. A founder on day 1 has none of this. We have zero empty/cold-start mockups across 14 pages.
Evidence: Workspace shows Emma's NDA 9 days late, Northzone in terms talks, £2.2k/unit BOM locked. Goal page has Sage's disprove test with 3 assumptions tagged Holding/Slipping/Broken. History shows 147 entries / 4-month reversal rate. Every mockup shows a working foundry; nobody has seen day-0.

What the founder actually sees on day 1

  1. Lands on Workspace → empty Strategy header ("What are you building?") + "Create your first Strategic Goal" CTA
  2. Opens Goal (but there isn't one yet) → nudged back to Workspace creation flow
  3. Opens Report → "No updates sent yet · talk to Cal to draft your first one"
  4. Opens History → "Nothing recorded yet · every decision you make here is logged"

What could break

  • Founder signs up · opens Workspace · sees something alien (Sarah Chen? who's Sarah?) · churns
  • Empty states are where product quality is weakest — and we've shown zero
  • Onboarding has no mockup. ~5-page flow MUST exist before code
Mitigation:
• Build the onboarding mockup — 5 pages: welcome · define purpose · create first Strategic Goal with Sage · invite your first fractional · tour the Workspace
• Build empty-state mockups for Workspace, Goal (no goals yet), Report (no updates), History (no entries) — four states, one page each or combined into toggles on existing pages
• Time-boxed starter data: first 7 days Sage + Cal help the founder populate via conversation, not forms
3

14 pages for one sidebar section is scope creepScope critique · net new to Plan

High
The Plan section redesign started as "collapse 9 pages into 3" in the original proposal. We now have 14 Plan mockups + a gap audit + ~12 gap-list additions = ~27 planned surfaces. That's more than Money (19). Plan is one of many ForgeOS sections. The redesign has lost its own thesis.
Evidence: PLAN-SECTION-REDESIGN-PROPOSAL.md opens with: "Nine pages collapse to three, with Today as the shared entry lens." 14 mockups later, we've added Report-Weekly, Report-Board, Objective-Create, Task-Create, Goal-Create, Pressure-Test, Gut-check, Delegate, History-Entry (pending). Each is valuable. Together they re-create the sprawl the redesign was supposed to kill.

Why this happened

  • Every click → a mockup: Tristan said "click through everything so I can see how it works", I built a page per click destination rather than using modals/drawers for some
  • Drill-down expanded: the original proposal said one /plan surface; we now have per-Goal drill-ins, per-Task drill-ins, per-specialist drill-ins, per-ritual drill-ins
  • Variants proliferated: one Report page became three target-pages; one pressure-test became its own page

What could break

  • Coding 14 full surfaces + onboarding + empty states + cross-cutting = 8-12 weeks of engineering for one sidebar section. Every section gets the same depth → years of work.
  • Users don't need this much. Most Plan work happens on Workspace + Goal + Report. The other 11 pages serve edge cases.
  • Each page adds schema + server actions + tests. The maintenance tail compounds.
Mitigation (MVP cut list):
• Ship V1 with 7-8 surfaces only: Workspace + Goal + Task + Report + History + Goal-Create + onboarding. Everything else modal-or-inline.
• Collapse the Report family: one Report page with a robust target picker; Weekly/Board are draft-preview states, not separate pages.
• Inline the Pressure-test: modal overlay from the Goal page, not a standalone route. Save the transcript to History as a record.
• Fold Delegate into Task / Goal / inline chat: no standalone chat page in V1. The conversation surface lives where the work is.
• Gut-check is a Goal-scoped page state, not a separate route (currently /plan/gutcheck).
4

The fractional-human pattern is applied unevenly across pagesConsistency critique · net new

Medium
After Tristan's feedback ("too AI-heavy in threads"), I introduced 5 fractional humans (Sarah / Emma / James / Priya / David) and rebalanced some mockups. But the rebalance is partial and inconsistent — some pages lead with humans; others still lead with specialists; the rule for "who authors vs who reviews" isn't formalised.
Evidence:
  • Workspace: 3 of 7 tasks swapped to humans (Emma/James/Priya); 4 kept specialist-lead. No rule for why these 3.
  • Goal page: disprove test still attributed to Sage (AI), not Sarah (who would own Fundraise). Team composition card exists only on Workspace.
  • Objective-Create: Sage solo-drafts. Sarah isn't in the loop. But on Goal-Create, Sarah and Sage co-draft. Inconsistent.
  • Task detail: thread rebalanced well (Emma authors, Leo AI cross-checks). But parent Task summary still says "You + Emma" without the fractional pill consistently.
  • Report pages: Cal (AI) drafts all three targets. Sarah's round-size memo from the Delegate page could be an alternative human-drafted flow.

What could break

  • Users won't know the rule. Who drafts, who reviews, who owns?
  • Hiring real fractional humans is expensive. If the UI doesn't make "how to get value from them" obvious, founders will just use AI specialists and save money.
  • The feature ends up either over- or under-representing human work in copy surfaces (Report)
Mitigation:
• Formalise the authorship rule: humans author first drafts where domain-human exists for the Goal; AI specialist cross-checks / fact-verifies / summarises. Document in PLAN-MOCKUP-TRACKER.md.
• Sweep all 14 mockups to apply the rule consistently. Write a 10-minute audit checklist.
• Add Team-on-this-Goal card to Goal page (currently only on Workspace). Makes the human presence visible at every level.
• Reports: if Sarah is on Fundraise, her voice should lead the fundraise-relevant sections of the Monthly Investor update. Cal stitches together. Not Cal-drafts-everything.
5

The conversation-first creation pattern has no alternativeInteraction-model critique · net new

Medium
Every creation flow (Goal, Objective, Task) defaults to specialist-conversation + live-preview. It's a strong pattern — but it's the only pattern. Power-users, AI-averse founders, and bulk-entry cases have no escape hatch. If the conversation breaks (LLM offline, spec unclear), the founder is stuck.
Evidence: Goal-Create is a 3-participant chat (Tristan + Sarah + Sage). Objective-Create is Sage solo-chat. Task-Create is the exception — has a quick-add bar AND a full-form fallback. The other two don't. If the founder wants to type "add a goal: raise £750k, disprove test is X Y Z, milestone 31 Jul, done" into a form, there's no affordance.

Real founder usage patterns we're not serving

  • Bulk setup: founder who plans on a Sunday and wants to add 8 tasks in 5 minutes — conversation is too slow
  • AI-averse: founder who distrusts LLMs will never use the chat creation flow. They'll abandon rather than persist
  • LLM down: infrastructure failure shouldn't block the core feature. Fallback must exist.
  • Duplicate / template: copying a previous Goal's structure is faster than re-drafting via chat — no pattern exists
Mitigation:
• Add "direct entry" toggle to Goal-Create and Objective-Create: switch to a classic form. Both exist side-by-side; conversation is default, form is one click away.
• Mock the specialist-offline state: when Sage is unavailable, show the form as the primary mode + clear note "Sage is back in ~4 min · or fill in directly below".
• Add template library: Q2 Fundraise Goal template, Pilot Delivery Goal template, etc. · picker appears alongside the chat in create flows. Bulk setup time drops from 20 min to 3.
• Duplicate pattern: "Create Goal from previous" — clone a completed/archived Goal, edit dates and team.
