# Automated Indoor Mushroom Farm Brief

We are designing an automated indoor mushroom growing facility — a modular, climate-controlled fungiculture system that takes pasteurised substrate through spawning, incubation and fruiting to harvest, with automated racking, climate management and irrigation. Unlike leafy-greens vertical farming, mushroom cultivation runs largely in the dark, demands very high humidity, and must continuously vent the carbon dioxide the mushrooms respire rather than dose it. The facility produces a steady weekly yield of fresh gourmet mushrooms (oyster and shiitake) for regional food service and retail.

Target market: UK and EU controlled-environment-agriculture operators, food cooperatives and specialist growers seeking year-round, weather-independent fresh-mushroom production close to urban demand, with consistent quality and low labour through automation. Single-facility sale ex-works; the growing rooms ship as insulated modular panels and are assembled inside the customer's building shell or a series of linked containers.

Process description (batch-continuous, staggered):
- Receive and pasteurise/sterilise substrate (straw, sawdust, supplemented hardwood); cool to spawning temperature
- Inoculate (spawn) the substrate with grain spawn in a clean spawning area; fill into grow bags or blocks
- Incubate the spawned substrate in a warm, dark colonisation room (~24 °C) until the mycelium fully runs through the substrate
- Move colonised blocks to fruiting chambers held at high humidity, lower temperature and elevated fresh-air exchange to trigger and grow fruiting bodies
- Mist and irrigate the fruiting chambers automatically; manage humidity, temperature, CO2 and light on a per-chamber recipe
- Harvest in successive flushes; move spent substrate to a composting/disposal route
- Pack and cool fresh mushrooms for dispatch

Key constraints:
- Primary output target: 500 kg/week of fresh mushrooms (≈ 26 tonnes/year), staggered across fruiting chambers for a steady daily harvest
- Growing area: approximately 210 m² of multi-tier shelf area across colonisation and fruiting rooms
- Crop: gourmet mushrooms (oyster and shiitake); 14-21 day cycle from spawned block to first flush, multiple flushes per block
- Climate per fruiting chamber: 16-18 °C, 85-95% relative humidity, CO2 held below 800-1,000 ppm by fresh-air exchange (CO2 venting, NOT dosing), low-level timed LED light for fruiting-body morphology
- Climate per colonisation room: ~24 °C, moderate humidity, dark
- Substrate throughput: approximately 1.2 tonnes/week of pasteurised substrate
- Automation: automated mobile racking / shelf movement, per-chamber misting and irrigation, CO2 and humidity control, environmental SCADA with per-recipe chamber control, and harvest-area conveyance
- Water and nutrient: misting and irrigation fed from a dosing and filtration skid; condensate and run-off managed and drained
- Design life: 15 years for the structure and racking; food-contact-grade, washdown-capable internal surfaces
- Operating environment: indoor; rooms held at recipe setpoints; external ambient -5 °C to +35 °C handled by the HVAC plant; high internal humidity demands corrosion-resistant fittings and condensation management
- Unit cost ceiling: £760,000 ex-works for the complete facility (insulated modular grow rooms, multi-tier automated racking, HVAC and humidity plant, fresh-air-exchange and CO2-control system, lighting, irrigation/misting and dosing skid, substrate pasteuriser, controls and packing-area fit-out) — excludes the host building shell and external services
- Annual production volume: 8 facilities per year
- Jurisdiction: UK

Safety and regulatory:
- UKCA and CE marking
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC for the automated racking, conveyors, pasteuriser and dosing equipment
- EMC Directive 2014/30/EU and Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU for the electrical and control systems
- Food hygiene: Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 and food-contact-material compliance for harvested-product surfaces
- HACCP-based food-safety plan for the growing and packing process
- BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations) for the electrical installation in a high-humidity washdown environment, including IP-rated fittings and RCD protection
- COSHH for substrate pasteurisation chemicals, cleaning agents and mushroom-spore exposure (respiratory protection in spawning and harvest areas)
- F-Gas Regulation for the refrigeration/HVAC refrigerant charge
- Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations for the irrigation supply and backflow prevention

Sub-modules expected: insulated modular grow-room enclosures (colonisation + fruiting), multi-tier automated mobile racking, HVAC and dehumidification plant, fresh-air-exchange and CO2-control system, humidity/misting generation, low-level LED fruiting lighting, irrigation and nutrient dosing/filtration skid, substrate pasteuriser/steriliser and spawning area, environmental SCADA with per-chamber recipe control, harvest conveyance and packing/cooling area, washdown drainage and condensate management, and the electrical distribution and control panel.
