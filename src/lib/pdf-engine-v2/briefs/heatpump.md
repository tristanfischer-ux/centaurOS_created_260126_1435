# Heat Pump Test Brief

We are developing a 30 kW monobloc air-to-water heat pump using R290 (propane) refrigerant, for UK commercial retrofit into existing hydronic heating circuits.

Target customer: UK small-commercial buildings (offices 500-2,000 m², light industrial, hospitality) replacing gas boilers during net-zero refurbishments. Installed and commissioned by a Heat Pump Association (HPA) registered contractor in one working day.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £7,500 ex-works (MCS-accreditable unit cost, not installed price)
- Target unit mass: ≤ 180 kg for two-person lift + trolley placement
- External envelope: 1,100 × 450 × 1,300 mm (W × D × H) — designed to fit beside existing plant-room condensing boilers
- Heat output: 30 kW nominal at A7/W55 per EN 14825
- Seasonal COP (SCOP): ≥ 3.5 at low-temperature application zone H3 (UK average)
- Flow temperature: up to 65 °C for legacy radiator compatibility
- Refrigerant charge: ≤ 500 g R290 to meet EN 378 restricted-charge outdoor installation rules
- Operating ambient: -20 °C to +40 °C, with capacity maintained to -7 °C
- Acoustic target: ≤ 52 dBA at 1 m outdoor-side sound pressure level, low-noise mode available for plant-room installation
- Annual batch size: 500 units per year

Safety and regulatory:
- EN 378 (refrigeration system safety, R290 charge limits)
- EN 14825 (performance rating and SCOP)
- PED 2014/68/EU (pressure equipment directive, applicable to refrigerant circuit)
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC
- MCS MIS 3005 (UK micro-generation accreditation)
- F-Gas Regulation (EU) 517/2014 — R290 is a natural refrigerant and exempt from phase-down, but installers must be F-Gas trained

Sub-modules expected: compressor (scroll or rotary, R290-rated), brazed-plate heat exchanger evaporator, brazed-plate condenser, electronic expansion valve, variable-speed fan + coil, circulation pump, controller + inverter drive, enclosure + acoustic insulation, safety/leak detection.
