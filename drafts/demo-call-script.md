# ForgeOS Demo Call Script (20 minutes)

## Before the call
- Open ForgeOS in a browser tab, logged in, with Today page visible
- Have the prospect's LinkedIn/company page open for reference
- Know what product they're building (from outreach research)
- Have Investor search, CAD Lab, and Cash Burn pages ready in separate tabs

---

## 1. Discovery (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before I show you anything — tell me about [product]. Where are you in the journey right now?"

**Listen for:**
- What stage they're at (idea, prototype, production)
- What's blocking them (fundraising, engineering, strategy, suppliers, team)
- How they're currently solving the problem (freelancers, agencies, doing it all themselves)

> "Got it. So the main challenges are [reflect back their pain]. Let me show you how ForgeOS handles all of that in one place."

---

## 2. Product Overview (3 min)

> "ForgeOS is the operating system for hardware startups. Everything you need to go from idea to investable, manufacturing-ready company — in one platform."

Walk through the platform at a high level:
- **Today page:** "This is your command centre — objectives, tasks, key metrics at a glance"
- **13 specialists:** "You have a full team on demand — strategy, CTO, VP manufacturing, finance, legal, sales, marketing, fundraising, and more"
- **Key tools:** "Investor search, concept packages, manufacturer marketplace, cash burn, board packs"

> "Think of it as having the full support team of a well-funded startup — without the headcount or the payroll."

---

## 3. Investor Search (3 min)

> "You mentioned fundraising — let me show you something. We have 7,800+ investors profiled with stage, sector, cheque size, and contact details. Plus 49,000+ contacts and 3,000+ grants."

**Do this live:**
1. Open Investor Search
2. Filter by their stage (pre-seed/seed) and sector (whatever matches their product)
3. Show the matched investors with key details

> "Instead of spending weeks building a target list from Crunchbase and LinkedIn, you search here and get matched investors in seconds. Each profile includes what they've funded before, their typical cheque size, and how to reach them."

---

## 4. Specialists (3 min)

> "Let me show you the specialists. You have 13 domain experts available anytime."

1. Open a specialist relevant to their biggest pain point (e.g., Fiona for fundraising, Sage for strategy, Max for engineering)
2. Ask a question relevant to their product
3. Show the quality and depth of the response

> "Every specialist knows your project context. They don't just give generic advice — they tailor it to your product, your stage, your market. And importantly, they support YOUR decisions — they don't replace your judgment."

---

## 5. CAD Lab — Concept Package (3 min)

> "Now let me show you concept packages. I'm going to type in a description of a product similar to yours and show you what comes out."

**Do this live:**
1. Open CAD Lab → New Project
2. Type a product description inspired by THEIR product (use their language)
3. Click generate
4. While it processes: "This normally takes 2-3 minutes. What you're about to see would take a freelance engineer 4-8 weeks and cost £5,000-£15,000."

Walk through the output:
- **Architecture:** "Modular design with component breakdown"
- **BOM:** "Full bill of materials with costs at 1K, 10K, and 100K units"
- **DFM Analysis:** "Manufacturing recommendations — [point out something relevant]"
- **Matched Suppliers:** "UK suppliers matched from 13,700+ manufacturers. One-click RFQs."

---

## 6. Strategy & Tasks (2 min)

> "ForgeOS isn't just tools — it helps you run your startup day to day."

1. Show the Strategy/Objectives view: "Set your company objectives, break them into tasks, assign to team members or specialists"
2. Show Cash Burn: "Quick runway view — see your burn rate, when you need to raise, how much you need"

> "Board packs, P&L projections, team management — it's all here. One platform instead of twelve different tools."

---

## 7. Pricing & Close (2 min)

> "So here's the pricing. The Startup Team plan is £49/month — that gives you 100 AI tasks, full access to all 13 specialists, investor search, CAD Lab, manufacturer marketplace, and all the business tools."

> "For context: a fractional CTO costs £1,000-£2,000/month. A VP Manufacturing, same. A finance consultant, same. For £49/month you get all of them — plus investor intelligence and manufacturing connections."

**The ask:**

> "Would you like me to set up your account? I'll walk you through your first real project this week — we can run your actual product through the concept package and find investors matching your stage and sector."

**If yes:** Help them sign up right on the call, or send the link immediately after.

**If not yet:**

> "Totally understand. Here's what I'd suggest — try the free trial for 14 days. Put YOUR product and YOUR fundraising targets through the platform. If it's useful, the £49/month is a fraction of what any single one of these services would cost. I'll check in with you on Thursday."
