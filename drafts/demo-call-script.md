# ForgeOS Demo Call Script (20 minutes)

## Before the call
- Open ForgeOS in a browser tab, logged in, with CAD Lab ready
- Have the prospect's LinkedIn/company page open for reference
- Know what product they're building (from outreach research)

---

## 1. Discovery (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time, [Name]. Before I show you anything — tell me about [product]. Where are you in the journey right now?"

**Listen for:**
- What stage they're at (idea, prototype, production)
- What's blocking them (finding suppliers, engineering costs, time)
- How they're currently solving the problem (freelancers, agencies, DIY)

> "Got it. So the main bottleneck is [reflect back their pain]. Let me show you exactly how ForgeOS handles that."

---

## 2. Live CAD Lab Demo (5 min)

> "I'm going to type in a description of a product similar to yours and show you what comes out."

**Do this live:**
1. Open CAD Lab → New Project
2. Type a product description inspired by THEIR product (use their language)
3. Click generate
4. While it processes: "This normally takes 2-3 minutes. What you're about to see would take a freelance engineer 4-8 weeks and cost £5,000-£15,000."

> "What's happening is 13 AI specialists are working together — a CTO designing the architecture, a VP Manufacturing checking DFM, a supply chain lead matching suppliers."

---

## 3. Engineering Package Review (5 min)

Walk through each tab:
- **Architecture:** "Here's the modular design it's proposed — [describe key decisions]"
- **BOM:** "Full bill of materials with costs at three volumes — 1,000, 10,000, and 100,000 units"
- **DFM Analysis:** "These are the manufacturing recommendations — [point out something relevant to their product]"
- **Matched Suppliers:** "47 UK suppliers matched based on capability. You can send RFQs to any of them with one click."

> "[Name], does this look like the kind of output your engineering team would produce? Because this took 3 minutes."

---

## 4. Supplier Marketplace (3 min)

> "Let me show you the marketplace — we have 13,700+ UK manufacturers."

1. Search for a category relevant to their product
2. Show supplier cards with certifications, location, company size
3. Show the RFQ flow: "One click, your specs go to the supplier with full context"

> "The key difference from Xometry or Geomiq: we don't just connect you with suppliers — we generate the engineering package they need to quote accurately. That means faster, more precise quotes."

---

## 5. AI Specialists (3 min)

> "One more thing — you get a virtual C-suite. Let me show you."

1. Open a specialist chat (e.g., Max the CTO or Fang VP Manufacturing)
2. Ask a question relevant to their product
3. Show the quality and depth of the response

> "You can ask any of these specialists anything, anytime. They know your project context, your industry, your constraints."

---

## 6. Pricing & Close (2 min)

> "So here's the pricing. The Startup Team plan is £49/month — that gives you 100 AI tasks, full CAD Lab access, marketplace access, and all 13 specialists."

> "For context, one freelance engineering package costs £5,000-£15,000. You get unlimited iterations for £49/month."

**The ask:**

> "I'm onboarding the first 100 founding members personally. Right now we're at [X] of 100. Would you like me to set up your account? I'll walk you through your first real engineering package this week."

**If yes:** Help them sign up right on the call, or send the link immediately after.

**If not yet:** 

> "Totally understand. Here's what I'd suggest — try the free trial for 14 days. Put YOUR product through the CAD Lab. If the engineering package is useful, the £49/month is a no-brainer. I'll check in with you on Thursday."

---
