# UK 200 L Research-Scale Single-Use Bioreactor Brief

We are developing a 200 L single-use bioreactor (SUB) for pilot-scale process development and clinical-grade Phase 1/2a manufacture of monoclonal antibodies and cell-therapy products. Target volume is research-and-development scale — smaller than the 2,000 L commercial production unit but with the same instrumentation feature set, suitable for academic biomanufacturing centres and small-molecule biotech labs.

Target customer: UK and EU academic biomanufacturing centres (Cell and Gene Therapy Catapult at Stevenage, University of Sheffield AMRC Biomanufacturing, ETH Zurich Process Development), and early-stage biotechs (£5-20 M Series A) running first-in-human Phase 1/2a trials. Operated under EU GMP Annex 1 (revised August 2023) and FDA 21 CFR 210/211 for clinical-grade material.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £180,000 ex-works for the stainless-steel containment hardware + base instrumentation (excluding consumable bag, advanced sensors, and validation services). Single-use bag price tracked separately at £1,200 per bag.
- Nominal working volume: 200 L (sized for 120-180 L process inventory at 60-90% of nominal)
- Mixing: single three-blade impeller, top-driven via magnetic coupling (sterility-preserving), tip-speed 0.8-2.0 m/s
- Mass transfer coefficient: kLa ≥ 8 hr⁻¹ at 1 vvm air sparging
- Gas exchange: 4-gas mass-flow controllers (air, oxygen, nitrogen, carbon dioxide), micro-sparger, dissolved-oxygen setpoint 30-60% saturation
- Temperature control: heating/cooling jacket loop (5-45 °C controllable, ±0.2 °C stability at 37 °C); resistance-element heater + chiller circulator
- Process analytical: pH, dissolved oxygen, dissolved carbon dioxide, temperature, biomass capacitance, optical-density turbidity, Raman spectroscopy port (optional)
- Footprint: ≤ 1.4 m diameter × 2.4 m tall (Class C cleanroom corridor pass-through limit, single-operator skid-mounted)
- Cleanroom compatibility: ISO 14644-1 Class 7 (Grade C) external surfaces, mirror-polished 316L stainless to Ra ≤ 0.4 µm
- Annual production target: 30 units per year by year 4 (UK + EU market)
- Validation timeline: 8-week installation qualification, operational qualification, performance qualification (IQ/OQ/PQ) pack delivered with every unit

Safety and regulatory:
- EU GMP Annex 1 (Sterile Manufacture, 2023 revision) — contamination control strategy
- EU GMP Annex 11 (Computerised Systems) — automation 21 CFR Part 11 compliance
- ICH Q7 (Good Manufacturing Practice for Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients)
- ASME Bioprocessing Equipment (BPE-2022) — surface finish, drainability, cleanability
- ISO 14644-1:2015 (cleanroom classification)
- Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU (PED) — Category I or II depending on temperature/pressure
- IEC 61511 (functional safety for process industries) — SIL-2 for jacket overpressure shutdown
- BS EN 1672-2:2020 (hygienic-design machinery)
- USP <87> / <88> (biocompatibility of the contact-surface plastics — vendor responsibility)

Sub-modules expected: stainless-steel containment vessel (316L mirror-polished with cradle and load-cells), single-use bag interface (irradiation-tolerant polyethylene with integral ports for sensors, gas, harvest, and sampling), top-mounted impeller drive (magnetic coupling + variable-frequency drive motor), gas-sparging system (mass-flow-controller bank + sparger ring + filter housing), temperature-control loop (jacket pump, heater, chiller plate), process-analytical instrumentation port array (pH, dissolved-oxygen, biomass, temperature probes via single-use ports), automated control system (programmable logic controller + supervisory control and data acquisition with electronic batch record), cleaning utility connections (purified water, clean steam, although bag is single-use the housing requires periodic cleaning).
