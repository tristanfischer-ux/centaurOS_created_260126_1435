# Vertical Farm Test Brief

We are designing a modular indoor vertical farm unit for leafy-greens production, intended to be stood inside a standard 40-foot refrigerated shipping container or deployed in light industrial space. Each unit is sold as a self-contained growing system with environmental control and fertigation integrated.

Target customer: small UK food producers, supermarket own-label pilots, corporate cafeterias, and restaurant groups who want a 100-250 m² indoor farming footprint producing 150-300 kg of leafy greens per week year-round.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £55,000 ex-works
- Growing footprint: 12 m² per unit (60 growing trays across 6 vertical tiers, each 1.2 × 1.0 m)
- External envelope: 2,400 × 1,400 × 2,700 mm (W × D × H), two-person assembly on site
- Target yield: 12-15 kg leafy greens per week per unit at steady state
- Crop types: lettuce, basil, rocket, pak choi, kale (no fruiting crops in v1)
- Energy use: ≤ 2.5 kWh per kg of produce at steady state
- Water use: ≤ 1.5 L per kg produce (closed-loop recirculating deep-water culture)
- Lighting: tunable-spectrum horticultural LED at 200-350 µmol·m⁻²·s⁻¹ PPFD per tier
- Climate: 20-24 °C, 60-75% RH, 800-1,200 ppm CO₂ (dosed from cylinder)
- Annual batch size: 100 units per year

Safety and regulatory:
- BS EN 60204-1 (electrical safety of machinery)
- EN 60335-2-76 (safety of water-contact appliances, applicable to nutrient pumps)
- Food contact materials — BS EN 1186 / EU 10/2011 for any plastic in direct contact with irrigation water
- BRCGS Global Standard for Agriculture (for customers selling into supermarkets)
- WRAS approval for any mains water connection components
- CE / UKCA marking for completed product

Sub-modules expected: growing rack frame + trays, LED lighting array + drivers, fertigation tank + pumps + dosing, circulation fans + HEPA filter, HVAC / dehumidifier, CO₂ dosing, environmental sensor stack (temp, RH, CO₂, EC, pH, PAR), PLC + HMI, power distribution and safety.
