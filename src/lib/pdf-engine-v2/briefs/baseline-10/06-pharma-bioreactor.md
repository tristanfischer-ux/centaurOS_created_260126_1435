# Single-Use Pharma Bioreactor Brief

We are designing a 200 L single-use bioreactor skid for mammalian cell-culture expression of monoclonal antibodies, aimed at contract development and manufacturing organisations (CDMOs) and smaller biotechs running Phase I and II clinical supply campaigns in UK and EU cleanrooms.

Target market: UK CDMOs (Fujifilm Diosynth, Cobra Biologics, Oxford Biomedica contract arm) and early-stage biotechs running 50-500 L perfusion or fed-batch campaigns at ISO 7 / Grade B cleanroom class. Competes with Sartorius Biostat STR 200, Thermo Fisher HyPerforma S.U.B. 200 L and Cytiva Xcellerex XDR-200.

Key constraints:
- Unit cost ceiling: £78,000 ex-works for the stainless-steel skid (single-use bags priced separately on consumable model)
- Bag working volume: 100 L to 200 L (2:1 turn-down)
- External envelope: 1,200 × 1,100 × 2,100 mm (W × D × H), single operator loading height ≤ 1.5 m
- Mixing: bottom-mounted magnetic-drive impeller, tip-speed 0.5 to 2.5 m/s
- Gas transfer (kLa): ≥ 30 / hour at 100 L working volume
- Sparging: macro + micro ring spargers, air / O₂ / N₂ / CO₂ mass-flow controllers 0-10 slpm each
- Temperature control: 22 to 42 °C ± 0.2 °C via electric jacket + chilled-water loop
- pH control: 6.5 to 7.6 ± 0.05, via CO₂ + 1 M sodium hydroxide feed
- DO control: 20 to 60 % air saturation ± 5 %
- Stainless-steel contact surfaces: 316L electropolished to Ra ≤ 0.5 µm; non-contact frames 304
- Annual production volume: 250 skids per year

Safety and regulatory:
- USP Class VI biocompatibility for single-use materials
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records and electronic signatures
- EU GMP Annex 1 (2022 revision) manufacture of sterile medicinal products
- EU GMP Annex 15 qualification and validation
- ASME BPE bioprocessing equipment standards
- MHRA Good Manufacturing Practice for a Life Sciences exporter
- ATEX directive 2014/34/EU — Zone 2 certification not required if bag integrity maintained
- Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC
- Ingress protection IP54; all product-contact components gamma-irradiation validated to 25-50 kGy

Sub-modules expected: 316L stainless-steel skid frame with levelling feet and castors, 200 L bioprocess-bag holder with removable cradle, bottom-mount magnetic impeller drive + servomotor, electric jacket heater (10 kW) + chilled-water loop coil, gas mass-flow controller rack (air / O₂ / N₂ / CO₂), peristaltic feed pumps (pH base + acid + antifoam + glucose feed), single-use DO and pH sensor mountings with optical transmitters, 10 kg weigh cells for integrated inventory, 15-inch HMI touchscreen running a GAMP 5 validated recipe engine, PLC + 21 CFR Part 11 audit log, cleanroom-grade HEPA filter on the gas exhaust, single-use sampling and harvest port manifold.
